02 / RECORDED RETAIL EVIDENCE

Price evidence is not proof of availability.

Retail observations retain their status and can be excluded from current benchmarking, used as historical references or flagged when availability conflicts. The chart below summarises 395 recorded observations, not distinct products or live stock checks.

  • 132 recorded current in-stock observations
  • 80 out-of-stock or preorder references
  • 37 conflicting-availability observations
  • 6 unverified-availability observations
  • 140 excluded observations

Recorded status from the reproducible 15 October dataset; actual stock may have changed since it was collected.

03 / PROCUREMENT SAFEGUARDS

Make buying constraints visible.
